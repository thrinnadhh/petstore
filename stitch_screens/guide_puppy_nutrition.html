Healthy Start

A comprehensive nutrition guide to build a strong foundation for your puppy's vital early weeks.

pie_chart Nutrient Targets

Essential building blocks for rapid growth and development.

High-Quality Protein 28% - 30%
Healthy Fats (DHA/EPA) 15% - 20%
Calcium to Phosphorus 1.2:1 Ratio

schedule Daily Feeding Rhythm

Morning (7:00 AM)

1/4 cup of moistened puppy kibble. Ensure fresh water is available.

Midday (12:00 PM)

1/4 cup kibble. Playtime should follow after 30 minutes to aid digestion.

Late Afternoon (4:00 PM)

1/4 cup kibble. Supervised feeding to monitor appetite.

Evening (8:00 PM)

Final 1/4 cup. Remove food bowl after 20 mins to help with house training overnight.

check_circle The Dos

  • done Soak kibble in warm water for 10 mins to make it easier for tiny teeth.
  • done Wash bowls thoroughly after every single meal to prevent bacteria.
  • done Stick to a strict schedule; routine aids digestion and potty training.

cancel The Don'ts

  • close Never feed table scraps; human food can be toxic and cause upset stomachs.
  • close Avoid 'free-feeding' (leaving food out all day). It makes monitoring intake hard.
  • close Don't supplement with extra calcium or vitamins without vet approval.
