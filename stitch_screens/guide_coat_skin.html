Coat & Skin Health

Health Guide

The Foundation of Radiance

A shiny coat isn't just about looks; it's a direct reflection of your dog's overall internal health and well-being.

Signs of a Healthy Coat

flare

Natural Shine

Hair should have a natural luster, indicating proper oil production from the skin.

touch_app

Smooth Texture

The coat should feel smooth and pliable, not brittle, coarse, or greasy to the touch.

clean_hands

Clean Skin

Skin beneath the fur should be clear, free of flakes (dandruff), bumps, or excessive redness.

psychology

No Odor

A healthy dog should not have a strong, lingering 'doggy' or yeasty smell immediately after washing.

set_meal Nutrition Focus

The Role of Omega-3s

Essential fatty acids, particularly Omega-3s (EPA and DHA), are crucial for managing inflammation and maintaining the skin's lipid barrier. They prevent dryness, reduce itching, and give the coat its characteristic shine.

  • check_circle Soothes dry, itchy skin
  • check_circle Reduces shedding and dander
warning

Common Irritants

  • Environmental Allergens Pollen, dust mites, and mold spores.
  • Harsh Shampoos Human shampoos strip natural oils. Use pH-balanced dog formulas.
  • Parasites Fleas and ticks cause severe allergic dermatitis.
